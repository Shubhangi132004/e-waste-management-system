E-waste awareness

Prepare Devices for Their Next Step

Check repair and reuse options, protect personal data, and use an authorised collection or recycling route.

🔧

Repair and reuse first

If a device is still useful, consider repair, refurbishment, donation, or resale before recycling.

  • Check whether the item can be repaired.
  • Remove SIM and memory cards.
  • Back up and erase personal data.
  • Keep chargers and devices labelled separately.
Use a trusted service provider for secure data erasure.
🔋

Handle batteries carefully

Keep loose batteries separate from general waste and use an appropriate collection point.

  • Do not puncture or dismantle batteries.
  • Do not put e-waste in household bins.
  • Keep damaged or swollen batteries away from heat.
  • Ask an authorised collector how to hand them over.
This project does not collect or transport batteries.
🏛️

Use official information

This student prototype is not a municipal or CPCB service. Check current official rules and authorised routes.

  • CPCB: E-Waste Management Rules
  • CPCB: E-Waste Rules FAQ (PDF)
  • Confirm local collection availability independently.
The external links are official references, not an integration.
